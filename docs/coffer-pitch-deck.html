01

NEAR Intents

One integration for asset discovery, exact cross-chain quotes, wallet-funded deposits and lifecycle status—without building a bridge for every corridor.

  • Invoice and payout settlement
  • Cross-chain treasury and payroll routing
  • 1Click production route; testnet adapter evidence
02

NEAR AI Cloud

Sensitive financial context is minimized, processed inside TEEs and accepted only when attestation, deployment identity and response signatures verify.

  • Private AI CFO analysis
  • Minimized payroll anomaly review
  • Fail-closed when verification is unavailable
03

NEAR Contracts

A factory creates a policy instance per business. Contracts bind immutable proposal digests and distinct approvals without holding treasury funds.

  • 2-of-N governance
  • Exact proposal digest approvals
  • No salary data or treasury custody on-chain
